## Lessons

  anywhere" pushed a worker to edit an accepted ADR's body; the requirement was mine, and the
  edit was reverted to an amendment.

  before any fix; two reviews' own numbers were wrong (the `delta` loss, the degrees of
  freedom) and one sub-claim was false.
