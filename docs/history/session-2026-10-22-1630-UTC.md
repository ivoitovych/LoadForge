## Lessons (journal §4.23, §5.13)

- A failure seen only under load or on one runner is a defect until its output says
  otherwise. Get the output first, reproduce under load, read the failing assertion.
- A test with no time limit hides its own failure for hours. A hang must fail fast and print.
- A thread that writes into a pipe must have a reader while it runs.
