## Lessons

- **Two degrees of freedom are combined, not minimised.** Taking the minimum is
  conservative. When the reference is noisy, it is far more conservative than the confidence
  level asked for.
- **A trailer is any `Word: text` line in the last paragraph.** The hook now checks the same
  thing CI does.
- **`(void)` does not silence `warn_unused_result` under GCC** (§4.27).
